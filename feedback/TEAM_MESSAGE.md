Subject: New in PEER — Contact and My Requests

Hi all,

From today PEER has a **Feedback** panel with two buttons:

- **Contact** — send me a request, an idea or a bug straight from Revit.
  If a PEER button fails, click Contact right after: the error is attached
  by itself.
- **My Requests** — see what happened to what you sent. Every request gets
  an answer within a week.

Why: I want to automate what costs us the most time, not what I happen to
notice. The "How often" and "Time per occurrence" fields in Contact are how
I pick.

What is collected: your request with the project and view name, and how
many times each PEER button is run and fails. Not your work in Revit, not
files or models. I look at it for the group, not per person.

One-page guide: feedback/USER_GUIDE.md in the PEER repository.

Thanks!
